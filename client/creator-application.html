CREATOR GUIDELINES

1. OWNERSHIP

You may only upload games that you created yourself or that you have explicit permission to distribute.

2. COPYRIGHT

Do not upload copyrighted games, music, artwork, fonts, or other assets unless you own the rights or have permission to use them.

3. MALWARE & SECURITY

Games must not contain malware, spyware, crypto miners, ransomware, or any software intended to harm users or their devices.

4. CONTENT POLICY

Do not publish illegal content, hate speech, explicit adult material, or content intended to harass or deceive other users.

5. QUALITY STANDARDS

Games should launch correctly, match their description, and provide a genuine playable experience.

6. WHAT WE ENCOURAGE

  • ✓ Indie Games
  • ✓ Student Projects
  • ✓ Retro-style Games
  • ✓ Game Jam Entries
  • ✓ Open Source Games
  • ✓ Experimental Projects

7. MODERATION

Retroholic reviews every creator application. We may reject or remove games that violate these guidelines or negatively affect the community.
